04 / semantics

One answer is a proof.
The other is a hypothesis.

A Bloom filter’s definitely-absent result is authoritative. Its maybe-present result needs context: accept probabilistic loss, verify against a source of truth, grow the structure, or constrain membership to an expiry window.
